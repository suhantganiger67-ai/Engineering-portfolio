# ⚙️ Fusion 360 CAD Engineering Portfolio

Welcome to my **CAD Engineering Portfolio**, where I document my journey of learning and developing skills in **Computer-Aided Design (CAD)** using **Autodesk Fusion 360**.

This repository contains a collection of approximately **20 self-created 3D models**, ranging from simple geometric objects to more complex shapes and mechanical components. Each model was created as a hands-on exercise to understand different modeling techniques and improve my ability to translate ideas and objects into accurate 3D designs.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills

* **Autodesk Fusion 360**
* 2D Sketching
* Geometric & Dimensional Constraints
* Parametric Modeling
* 3D Solid Modeling
* Extrude & Revolve
* Sweep & Loft
* Fillet & Chamfer
* Shell & Hole Features
* Pattern & Mirror
* Basic Mechanical Design
* Engineering Visualization

## 🎯 Purpose

The main purpose of this portfolio is to build a strong foundation in CAD through **consistent hands-on practice**.

Rather than following only tutorials, I have used different objects and shapes to experiment with Fusion 360's tools, understand how features interact, and develop better modeling habits.

Through these exercises, I am working on improving:

* Design accuracy
* Understanding of dimensions and constraints
* Feature selection
* Parametric modeling workflow
* Ability to model complex geometry
* Attention to detail
* Overall CAD workflow and confidence
